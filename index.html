Our Story

Austrian Excellence, Pakistani Soul

PAF-IAST is Pakistan's first applied sciences university modeled on the Austrian Fachhochschule system — purpose-built, industry-integrated, and internationally connected in Haripur, KPK.

  • Smart classrooms and advanced laboratories on a purpose-built green campus
  • Every faculty member trained in Austria or China — avg. USD 50,000 research grant
  • Mandatory industry internships every semester for real-world experience
  • Equal opportunity for all — women especially encouraged to join any program
  • Scholarships and financial aid for deserving students on merit and need basis
